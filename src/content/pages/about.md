# About

Founded in 2016, Studio Nusantara has completed **40+ built projects** across Indonesia — from family homes in Jakarta to hospitality projects in Bali and Lombok.

## Our approach

- **Place before style** — every project starts with climate, orientation, and local material
- **Client as collaborator** — decisions are made together, not presented as surprises
- **Buildability** — designs that contractors can actually build well, with local skills

## The team

Architects, interior designers, and project managers working across Indonesia and Southeast Asia — a lean team that keeps every project personal.
